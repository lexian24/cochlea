# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help.

## What is expected

- **Argue about the work, not the person.** Disagreement about a design is
  normal here and the spec exists to make it productive.
- **Say what you actually verified.** "I ran it and got X" and "I think X"
  are different claims, and conflating them wastes everyone's time.
- **Accept a "no, because F*n*" gracefully.** Many good ideas are declined
  because they reintroduce a failure mode. That is not a judgement of the
  idea or of you.
- **Be patient.** This is one person's project. Responses are not fast.

## What is not

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments, sustained disruption, or
publishing someone's private information. Anything that makes a reasonable
person not want to contribute.

## Reporting

Email the maintainer through their GitHub profile, or open a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/lexian24/cochlea/security/advisories/new)
if you would rather it not be public at all. Reports are handled by the
maintainer, who will also be the one who acts on them — there is no committee.

Consequences range from a request to stop, to having comments removed, to
being blocked from the repository. Which one depends on severity and on
whether it happens again.

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, discussions and
commits — and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.
